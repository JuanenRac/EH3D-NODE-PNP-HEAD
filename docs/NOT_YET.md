# EH3D-NODE-PNP-HEAD: not yet

Good ideas that are not in the current version. Writing them here keeps anybody from implementing them early or twice.

| Idea | Why not yet | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|
| The firmware for the board | The logic is written and tested first; the network, panel and update parts of the shared base are imported when the firmware is | the first board is chosen |
| Drivers for the pump, the valves and the sensors | They need the real parts on a bench to be tuned | a bench with one nozzle is there |
| Drivers for the Z and rotation motors with homing without end stops | They need the motors; the axes are a model today | the motors are chosen |
| The camera and the ring of light that look at the part | They belong to the camera node of the head | that node has a profile for a head |
| A paste dispenser | The decision about paste is open and it comes after the head works on a machine | the decision is taken |
| A nozzle changer and the sensors of the rack | It needs the changer; the pick-and-place program already models the tips | a changer is there |
